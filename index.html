About

How I work

Most videos lose people in the first second. That's where my job starts.

I work the whole chain myself — shot list, camera, edit, sound, colour, and the Arabic captions, which I typeset rather than auto-generate. Doing all of it means the idea doesn't get diluted on the way from the brief to the export.

Most of my work is vertical, because that's where my clients' audiences actually are. That's a constraint, not an excuse: the framing gets tighter, the cuts land earlier, and the story has to arrive faster than it would on a big screen. The pieces here were made for cafés, contractors, event halls, training institutes and a robotics company — different rooms, same question every time: what would make someone stay?
